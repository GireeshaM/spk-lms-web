Requirements

  • A basic understanding of computer graphics and design is recommended.

This course is divided into video tutorials, reading materials, and hands-on exercises. You will start with the basics and gradually move on to more advanced techniques, with lots of practical examples and real-life projects to help reinforce your learning.
